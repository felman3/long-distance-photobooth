How to play
  • Everyone floats down onto the island with an umbrella.
  • Throw water balloons to soak other players. Each splash makes them wetter; at zero dryness they're out.
  • Grab Soakers 🔫 for rapid fire, Mega balloons 🟣 for huge splashes, and Towels to dry off.
  • A rain storm shrinks the island. Stay inside the circle!
  • Computer: WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to throw, 1/2/3 or wheel to switch, Space to jump, Shift or right-click to dash, E to wave.
  • Phone: left thumb to walk, drag right thumb to aim and let go to throw. Tap the right side to throw at the nearest player.
